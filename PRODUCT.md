# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
## Product Purpose
A standalone portfolio demonstration of an apparel store. Products and prices are illustrative; orders are not accepted.
## Users
Inferred: visitors evaluating the portfolio and browsing a sample clothing collection on desktop or mobile.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Search, categories, product details, size and color selection, persistent local cart. Respect reduced motion. Independent visual identity from the grocery demo.
